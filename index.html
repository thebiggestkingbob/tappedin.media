The free media day

TAPPED IN will attend the agreed venue or event for one full day and create content during the experience. We decide what content is suitable for release on TAPPED IN's channels.

The released content will be posted through TAPPED IN and/or collaboratively with the venue, at TAPPED IN's discretion. The deliverable is the published content, not ownership of all media captured.

Want the media?

The free media day is designed to demonstrate what TAPPED IN can create. Content we choose to publish forms part of the TAPPED IN deliverable.

If you would like access to the captured media for your own channels, marketing or internal use, that media is available as a separately paid service.
